About CARVAN

Enriching minds and inspiring generations since 1946.

Our Legacy

Founded in 1946 by Chaudhry Abdul Hameed, CARVAN has grown into one of the nation's leading car marketplaces. For over seven decades, we have remained committed to providing quality vehicles and detailed car information to buyers, families, and auto enthusiasts alike.

Our Mission

To empower car buyers by providing accessible, authentic, and comprehensive car details that help them make informed choices.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative car marketplace in Pakistan, recognized for excellence, transparency, and customer satisfaction.

Why Choose CARVAN?

  • Legacy Since 1946: A tradition of quality and trust in vehicle trading.
  • Quality Assurance: Every vehicle undergoes detailed checks and verification.
  • Wide Reach: Access to cars across Pakistan with reliable information.
  • Commitment: We prioritize your needs at every step of your car buying journey.
